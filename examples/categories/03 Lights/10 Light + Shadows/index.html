Light + Shadows

Combine a directional light with a shadow generator, shadow casters, and a shadow receiver to create realistic shadows in a declarative 3D scene.

Light Shadows ShadowGenerator

What this demonstrates

A directional light can cast shadows from selected meshes onto a receiving surface.

Shadow Light

The shadow generator is connected to the shadowLight directional light through the light attribute.

Shadow Casters

The box, sphere, and cylinder are registered as shadow casters using their runtime references.

Shadow Receiver

The ground receives shadows through the arashtad-receiver element.

Shadow Settings

A 2048 shadow map and 0.42 darkness provide detailed, clearly visible shadows.

Source

The shadow configuration references the light and scene objects through the runtime registry. 

<arashtad-shadow
    light="shadowLight"
    mapSize="2048"
    darkness="0.42">

    <arashtad-caster ref="box">
    </arashtad-caster>

    <arashtad-caster ref="sphere">
    </arashtad-caster>

    <arashtad-caster ref="cylinder">
    </arashtad-caster>

    <arashtad-receiver ref="ground">
    </arashtad-receiver>

</arashtad-shadow>
