Light + Materials

Combine multiple lights with different material properties to show how diffuse, specular, emissive, and roughness settings affect a Babylon.js scene.

Light Materials StandardMaterial

What this demonstrates

Lighting and material properties work together to produce very different surface responses.

Diffuse

The diffuse material emphasizes the base surface color with a relatively low specular response.

Glossy

A low roughness value and bright specularColor produce stronger highlights from the directional lights.

Metallic Look

A dark diffuse surface combined with a strong specular response creates a reflective, metal-like appearance.

Emissive

emissiveColor adds a self-lit color contribution that remains visible alongside the scene lighting.

Source

Materials are created once and assigned to the declarative meshes through runtime references. 

<arashtad-light
    type="DirectionalLight"
    id="warmLight"
    args="new BABYLON.Vector3(-0.5, -0.85, 0.35)"
    position="new BABYLON.Vector3(6, 8, -5)"
    intensity="2.5"
    diffuse="new BABYLON.Color3(1, 0.3, 0.08)"
    specular="new BABYLON.Color3(0.5, 0.18, 0.05)">
</arashtad-light>

<arashtad-light
    type="DirectionalLight"
    id="coolLight"
    args="new BABYLON.Vector3(0.5, -0.75, -0.45)"
    position="new BABYLON.Vector3(-6, 6, 5)"
    intensity="2.2"
    diffuse="new BABYLON.Color3(0.08, 0.3, 1)"
    specular="new BABYLON.Color3(0.08, 0.16, 0.45)">
</arashtad-light>

<arashtad-material
    type="StandardMaterial"
    id="matGlossy"
    diffuseColor="new BABYLON.Color3(0.03, 0.2, 0.65)"
    specularColor="new BABYLON.Color3(0.9, 0.95, 1)"
    roughness="0.18">
</arashtad-material>

<arashtad-material
    type="StandardMaterial"
    id="matEmissive"
    diffuseColor="new BABYLON.Color3(0.02, 0.08, 0.06)"
    emissiveColor="new BABYLON.Color3(0.0, 0.65, 0.38)"
    specularColor="new BABYLON.Color3(0.12, 0.2, 0.16)"
    roughness="0.45">
</arashtad-material>

<arashtad-mesh
    type="Sphere"
    id="glossySphere"
    diameter="2.3"
    segments="48"
    position="new BABYLON.Vector3(-1.2, 1.15, 0)"
    material="matGlossy">
</arashtad-mesh>

<arashtad-mesh
    type="Torus"
    id="emissiveTorus"
    diameter="2.5"
    thickness="0.48"
    tessellation="48"
    position="new BABYLON.Vector3(3.7, 1.15, 0)"
    rotation="new BABYLON.Vector3(Math.PI / 2, 0, 0)"
    material="matEmissive">
</arashtad-mesh>
