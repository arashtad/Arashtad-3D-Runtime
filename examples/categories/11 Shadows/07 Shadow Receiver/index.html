Shadow Receiver

Configure a mesh as a shadow receiver for shadows generated by a Babylon.js ShadowGenerator.

Shadows Receiver
Shadow caster: model_1
Shadow receiver: ground

What this demonstrates

A runtime shadow receiver reference enables a mesh to display shadows generated by the configured light.

Receiver Reference

The receiver element references the ground mesh and enables it to receive shadows from the ShadowGenerator.

Shadow Surface

The ground remains a normal Babylon.js mesh while receiving the shadow projected by the model.

Source

Reference the ground mesh with arashtad-receiver to make it receive shadows. 

<arashtad-shadow
    light="directional"
    mapSize="2048"
    darkness="0.3"
    minZ="0.1"
    maxZ="100"
    orthoLeft="-20"
    orthoRight="20"
    orthoTop="20"
    orthoBottom="-20">

    <arashtad-generator
        useBlurExponentialShadowMap="true"
        blurKernel="64"
        blurScale="20">
    </arashtad-generator>

    <arashtad-caster ref="model_1"></arashtad-caster>
    <arashtad-receiver ref="ground"></arashtad-receiver>

</arashtad-shadow>
