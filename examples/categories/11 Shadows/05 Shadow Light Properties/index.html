143 · SHADOWS

Shadow Light Properties

Configure shadow-related properties on the light used by a ShadowGenerator.

Shadows Lighting
shadowMinZ: 0.1
shadowMaxZ: 100

What this demonstrates

Shadow-related properties can be applied directly to the light through the shadow configuration.

Shadow Range

The minZ and maxZ properties control the depth range used by the directional light for shadow calculations.

Light Configuration

Shadow-related light properties are configured through the shadow element while the underlying Babylon.js light remains accessible to the runtime.

Source

Configure the directional light's shadow range through the shadow element. 

<arashtad-shadow
    light="directional"
    mapSize="2048"
    darkness="0.3"
    minZ="0.1"
    maxZ="100"
    orthoLeft="-20"
    orthoRight="20"
    orthoTop="20"
    orthoBottom="-20">

    <arashtad-generator
        useBlurExponentialShadowMap="true"
        blurKernel="64"
        blurScale="20">
    </arashtad-generator>

    <arashtad-caster ref="model_1"></arashtad-caster>
    <arashtad-receiver ref="ground"></arashtad-receiver>

</arashtad-shadow>
