Shadows + Multiple Lights

Combine a shadow-producing directional light with an additional fill light to illuminate the same Babylon.js scene while preserving visible shadows.

Shadows Lighting Runtime
Shadow light: directional
Fill light: spot
Fill intensity: 0.1
Shadow caster: model_1
Shadow receiver: ground

What this demonstrates

A shadow-casting directional light paired with a non-shadow-casting fill light, so the scene is well lit without losing the visible shadow.

Shadow Light

The directional light provides primary illumination and is connected to the ShadowGenerator. The model casts its shadow onto the ground receiver.

Fill Light

A low-intensity SpotLight provides additional illumination without generating its own shadow map, keeping the directional shadow visible.

Source

Two lights share the scene. Only the directional light is wired to the shadow generator. 

<arashtad-light
    id="directional"
    type="DirectionalLight"
    direction="new BABYLON.Vector3(-0.5, -1, -0.5)"
    intensity="3"
>
</arashtad-light>

<arashtad-light
    id="spot"
    type="SpotLight"
    args="new BABYLON.Vector3(2, 5, 2), new BABYLON.Vector3(-0.3, -1, -0.3), Math.PI / 2, 2, scene"
    intensity="0.1"
>
</arashtad-light>

<arashtad-model
    id="model_1"
    src="../../../assets/3d/linux.glb"
>
</arashtad-model>

<arashtad-mesh
    type="Ground"
    id="ground"
    size="5, 5"
>
</arashtad-mesh>

<arashtad-shadow
    light="directional"
    mapSize="2048"
    darkness="0.3"
    minZ="0.1"
    maxZ="100"
    orthoLeft="-20"
    orthoRight="20"
    orthoTop="20"
    orthoBottom="-20"
>
    <arashtad-generator
        useBlurExponentialShadowMap="true"
        blurKernel="64"
        blurScale="20"
    ></arashtad-generator>

    <arashtad-caster ref="model_1"></arashtad-caster>
    <arashtad-receiver ref="ground"></arashtad-receiver>
</arashtad-shadow>
