Advanced Shadow Scene

Build a complete shadow scene with multiple shadow casters, a shared ShadowGenerator, configurable shadow quality, and multiple light sources.

Shadows Advanced Runtime
Shadow light: directional
Shadow casters: model_1, model_2
Shadow receiver: ground
Map size: 2048 × 2048
Shadow filter: Exponential blur

What this demonstrates

A single ShadowGenerator serving multiple casters, with per-generator quality settings and a shared receiving surface.

Multiple Casters

Multiple imported models can be registered with the same ShadowGenerator. Each caster contributes its own shadow to the shared receiving surface.

Advanced Generator

The generator combines a 2048 × 2048 shadow map with exponential shadow-map filtering and blur settings, while a separate low-intensity SpotLight provides additional scene illumination.

Source

Two models share one shadow generator, each contributing its own shadow to the ground receiver. 

<arashtad-light
    id="directional"
    type="DirectionalLight"
    direction="new BABYLON.Vector3(-0.5, -1, -0.5)"
    intensity="3"
>
</arashtad-light>

<arashtad-model
    id="model_1"
    src="../../../assets/3d/linux.glb"
    position="new BABYLON.Vector3(-0.5, 0.3, 0)"
>
</arashtad-model>

<arashtad-model
    id="model_2"
    src="../../../assets/3d/linux.glb"
    position="new BABYLON.Vector3(0.5, 0.3, 0)"
>
</arashtad-model>

<arashtad-mesh
    type="Ground"
    id="ground"
    size="6, 6"
    position="new BABYLON.Vector3(0, 0, 0)"
>
</arashtad-mesh>

<arashtad-shadow
    light="directional"
    mapSize="2048"
    darkness="0.3"
    minZ="0.1"
    maxZ="100"
    orthoLeft="-20"
    orthoRight="20"
    orthoTop="20"
    orthoBottom="-20"
>
    <arashtad-generator
        useBlurExponentialShadowMap="true"
        blurKernel="64"
        blurScale="20"
    ></arashtad-generator>

    <arashtad-caster ref="model_1"></arashtad-caster>
    <arashtad-caster ref="model_2"></arashtad-caster>

    <arashtad-receiver ref="ground"></arashtad-receiver>
</arashtad-shadow>
