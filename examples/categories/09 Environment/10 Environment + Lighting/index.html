Image-Based Environment + Scene Lighting

Environment + Lighting

120

Combines an HDR environment with explicit Babylon.js lights. The environment provides image-based lighting while additional lights provide direct illumination and control over the scene.

Environment + Direct Lighting The HDR environment supplies image-based illumination while the hemispheric and directional lights provide additional direct scene lighting.

Two Lighting Sources

An environment and explicit Babylon.js lights can operate together in the same scene.

Environment Lighting

The HDR environment contributes image-based lighting to the scene. Its strength is controlled by environmentIntensity.

The environment can also remain visible as the scene background through environmentBackground="true".

Explicit Lights

The scene also contains a HemisphericLight and a DirectionalLight.

These lights add direct illumination independently of the HDR environment.

Lighting Configuration

The environment and explicit lights have separate controls.

Environment

environment="../../../assets/environments/room.hdr" loads the HDR environment.

environmentIntensity="0.8" controls the strength of the environment lighting.

environmentBackground="true" displays the HDR environment around the objects.

Direct Lighting

The hemispheric light provides broad ambient-style illumination from its configured direction.

The directional light provides directional illumination with its own position, direction, and intensity.

Source

Complete environment and lighting configuration. 

<canvas
    id="environment-lighting-scene"
    clearColor="new BABYLON.Color4(0.025, 0.035, 0.032, 1)">

    <arashtad-runtime
        resize="engine.resize()"
        environment="../../../assets/environments/room.hdr"
        environmentIntensity="0.8"
        environmentBackground="true"
        environmentBackgroundBlur="0.12">
    </arashtad-runtime>

    <arashtad-camera
        id="camera"
        type="ArcRotateCamera"
        alpha="1.57"
        beta="1.15"
        radius="12"
        target="new BABYLON.Vector3(0, 1.2, 0)"
        lowerRadiusLimit="5"
        upperRadiusLimit="20"
        wheelDeltaPercentage="0.01">
    </arashtad-camera>

    <arashtad-light
        id="hemispheric-light"
        type="HemisphericLight"
        direction="new BABYLON.Vector3(0, 1, 0)"
        intensity="0.45">
    </arashtad-light>

    <arashtad-light
        id="directional-light"
        type="DirectionalLight"
        direction="new BABYLON.Vector3(-0.5, -1, -0.75)"
        position="new BABYLON.Vector3(4, 7, 4)"
        intensity="2.0">
    </arashtad-light>

    <arashtad-material
        id="metal-material"
        type="StandardMaterial"
        diffuseColor="new BABYLON.Color3(0.16, 0.18, 0.18)"
        specularColor="new BABYLON.Color3(1, 1, 1)">
    </arashtad-material>

    <arashtad-material
        id="accent-material"
        type="StandardMaterial"
        diffuseColor="new BABYLON.Color3(0.15, 0.64, 0.55)"
        specularColor="new BABYLON.Color3(0.8, 0.8, 0.8)">
    </arashtad-material>

    <arashtad-material
        id="dark-material"
        type="StandardMaterial"
        diffuseColor="new BABYLON.Color3(0.035, 0.045, 0.042)"
        specularColor="new BABYLON.Color3(0.45, 0.45, 0.45)">
    </arashtad-material>

    <arashtad-mesh
        type="Sphere"
        id="environment-sphere"
        diameter="2.8"
        segments="48"
        position="new BABYLON.Vector3(0, 1.8, 0)"
        material="metal-material">
    </arashtad-mesh>

    <arashtad-mesh
        type="Box"
        id="accent-box"
        width="2.2"
        height="2.2"
        depth="2.2"
        position="new BABYLON.Vector3(-3.2, 1.1, 0)"
        material="accent-material">
    </arashtad-mesh>

    <arashtad-mesh
        type="Cylinder"
        id="dark-cylinder"
        height="2.4"
        diameter="2.2"
        tessellation="48"
        position="new BABYLON.Vector3(3.2, 1.2, 0)"
        material="dark-material">
    </arashtad-mesh>

    <arashtad-mesh
        type="Ground"
        id="ground"
        width="14"
        height="14"
        position="new BABYLON.Vector3(0, -0.01, 0)"
        material="dark-material">
    </arashtad-mesh>

</canvas>
