Physics Shape Capsule

Use a CAPSULE physics shape for character-like bodies with Arashtad 3D Runtime and Havok physics.

Physics Shape Havok
type="CAPSULE" · character-like upright bodies falling onto the ground

What this demonstrates

Give a capsule-shaped mesh a matching CAPSULE physics shape, the standard collider for upright character bodies.

CAPSULE Shape

A capsule combines a cylinder with two hemispherical caps. Havok supports it as a first-class PhysicsShapeType, making it smooth and efficient for character-style collision.

Upright Bodies

With high friction and low restitution, the capsules land and settle upright on the ground rather than bouncing or sliding, which is exactly what a character controller needs.

Source

Match the mesh type with the physics shape type. Two capsules of different sizes show that the shape scales with the mesh.
