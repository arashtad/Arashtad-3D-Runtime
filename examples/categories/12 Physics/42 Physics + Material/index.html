193 · PHYSICS

Physics + Material

Assign different StandardMaterials to physics bodies with Arashtad 3D Runtime and Havok physics.

Physics Materials Physics
5 bodies · 4 StandardMaterials · shapes and colors combine freely

What this demonstrates

Materials and physics are orthogonal. Any mesh with any material can carry any physics shape, and multiple bodies can share a material.

Independent Concerns

The material attribute on a mesh points to an <arashtad-material>. The nested <arashtad-physics> element defines the body. Neither depends on the other.

Shape and Color Combinations

Five bodies, four materials. The orange material is reused on both a sphere and a flat box, showing that materials are shared resources rather than shape-specific.

Source

Declare each material once and reference it from any number of physics-enabled meshes. 

<arashtad-material
    id="green"
    type="StandardMaterial"
    diffuseColor="new BABYLON.Color3(0.3, 0.5, 0.3)"
>
</arashtad-material>

<arashtad-material
    id="orange"
    type="StandardMaterial"
    diffuseColor="new BABYLON.Color3(1.0, 0.4, 0.3)"
>
</arashtad-material>

<arashtad-material
    id="blue"
    type="StandardMaterial"
    diffuseColor="new BABYLON.Color3(0.3, 0.5, 0.9)"
>
</arashtad-material>

<arashtad-material
    id="yellow"
    type="StandardMaterial"
    diffuseColor="new BABYLON.Color3(0.9, 0.8, 0.3)"
>
</arashtad-material>

<arashtad-material
    id="brown"
    type="StandardMaterial"
    diffuseColor="new BABYLON.Color3(0.7, 0.5, 0.3)"
>
</arashtad-material>

<arashtad-mesh
    id="boxgreen"
    type="Box"
    size="1.2"
    position="new BABYLON.Vector3(-3, 3, -2)"
    material="green"
>
    <arashtad-physics
        type="BOX"
        mass="1"
        restitution="0.3"
        friction="0.5"
    ></arashtad-physics>
</arashtad-mesh>

<arashtad-mesh
    id="sphereorange"
    type="Sphere"
    diameter="1.2"
    segments="24"
    position="new BABYLON.Vector3(0, 3, -2)"
    material="orange"
>
    <arashtad-physics
        type="SPHERE"
        mass="1"
        restitution="0.4"
        friction="0.4"
    ></arashtad-physics>
</arashtad-mesh>
