Stacked Boxes Tower

Stack several dynamic boxes and watch the tower topple using Arashtad 3D Runtime and Havok physics.

Physics Multiple Stacking
4-box tower · click the sphere to launch it into the stack

What this demonstrates

A stack of dynamic bodies held together by friction and a heavy projectile that knocks the tower apart on command.

Stack Stability

Low restitution and high friction on each box keep the stack standing even though every box is fully dynamic. That is the same recipe Havok recommends for stable stacks.

Click to Launch

A heavy sphere sits to the left of the stack with an onPickTrigger that sets its linear velocity toward the tower. Clicking it fires the cannonball and topples the stack.

Source

Stack boxes with matched friction and restitution, then use a click trigger to launch a projectile into the tower. 

<arashtad-mesh
    id="b1"
    type="Box"
    size="1"
    position="new BABYLON.Vector3(0, 0.5, 0)"
    material="green"
>
    <arashtad-physics
        type="BOX"
        mass="1"
        restitution="0.05"
        friction="0.8"
    ></arashtad-physics>
</arashtad-mesh>

<!-- Repeat for b2 at y=1.5, b3 at y=2.5, b4 at y=3.5 -->

<arashtad-mesh
    id="projectile"
    type="Sphere"
    diameter="0.8"
    segments="24"
    position="new BABYLON.Vector3(-6, 2.5, 0)"
    material="green"
    onPickTrigger="
        const aggregate = target.metadata?.physicsAggregate;
        aggregate?.body?.setLinearVelocity(
            new BABYLON.Vector3(15, 0, 0)
        );
    "
>
    <arashtad-physics
        type="SPHERE"
        mass="3"
        restitution="0.2"
        friction="0.4"
    ></arashtad-physics>
</arashtad-mesh>
