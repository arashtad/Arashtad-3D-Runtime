Angry Ball Launcher

Aim and launch spheres at a stack of boxes with a GUI trigger using Arashtad 3D Runtime and Havok physics.

Physics Advanced Interaction

Aim

ready · 8 targets
Adjust angle and power · click "Launch ball" to fire a projectile at the stack

What this demonstrates

A projectile launcher with live angle and power controls, a target stack of stacked bodies, and cleanup on reset.

Aim Controls

Two range sliders set the launch angle and speed. Each launch spawns a fresh ball at the left edge of the scene and gives it a velocity along the chosen direction.

Target Stack

Eight dynamic boxes are arranged in a pyramid on a static platform. The stack stays standing thanks to high friction and low restitution; a well-aimed ball knocks it down.

Source

Sliders set the launch vector, the button spawns a ball with that velocity, and the reset button disposes spawned balls and rebuilds the stack. 

<input type="range" id="angle-slider" min="-45" max="45" value="0" step="1">
<input type="range" id="power-slider" min="5" max="30" value="18" step="1">

<button type="button" id="launch-button">Launch ball</button>
<button type="button" id="reset-button">Reset stack</button>
