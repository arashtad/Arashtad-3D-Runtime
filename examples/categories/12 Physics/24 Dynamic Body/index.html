Dynamic Body

Create a dynamic body that responds to gravity and collisions with Arashtad 3D Runtime and Havok physics.

Physics Body Dynamic
box1 · mass="1" · box2 · mass="5" · both dynamic bodies under gravity

What this demonstrates

Any body with a positive mass is dynamic. It falls under gravity, collides with other bodies, and reacts to forces and impulses.

Positive Mass

Setting mass above zero makes the body dynamic. Havok applies gravity to it every step and resolves any contacts it enters.

Mass Comparison

Both boxes fall at the same rate under gravity, but the heavier body is harder to move on contact. That is exactly the difference mass encodes in Havok.

Source

Use a positive mass to make a body dynamic. Different masses show that dynamic behavior is defined by mass being greater than zero. 

<arashtad-material
    id="green"
    type="StandardMaterial"
    diffuseColor="new BABYLON.Color3(0.3, 0.5, 0.3)"
>
</arashtad-material>

<arashtad-material
    id="orange"
    type="StandardMaterial"
    diffuseColor="new BABYLON.Color3(1.0, 0.4, 0.3)"
>
</arashtad-material>

<arashtad-material
    id="brown"
    type="StandardMaterial"
    diffuseColor="new BABYLON.Color3(0.7, 0.5, 0.3)"
>
</arashtad-material>

<arashtad-mesh
    id="ground"
    type="Ground"
    width="14"
    height="14"
    material="brown"
>
    <arashtad-physics
        type="BOX"
        mass="0"
        friction="0.8"
    ></arashtad-physics>
</arashtad-mesh>

<arashtad-mesh
    id="box1"
    type="Box"
    size="1"
    position="new BABYLON.Vector3(-2, 3, 0)"
    material="green"
>
    <arashtad-physics
        type="BOX"
        mass="1"
        restitution="0.3"
        friction="0.5"
    ></arashtad-physics>
</arashtad-mesh>
