Shape selector · spawn button · pause and reset controls · HDR environment

What this demonstrates

All the runtime's main physics capabilities composed into one scene: static and dynamic bodies, multiple shapes, mixed Standard and PBR materials, an HDR environment, and interactive UI controls.

Scene Composition

The bowl, tower base, and ramp are static. The two boxes and metallic ball that fall at load time are dynamic. Spawned bodies join them from a menu of four shapes.

Live Controls

The right-hand panel spawns shapes, pauses simulation, and resets the scene. Every control operates on the same shared physics context, so the effects compose cleanly.

Source

Static walls and platforms, dynamic bodies in markup, and host-page controls for spawning, pausing, and resetting. 

<arashtad-mesh id="ground" type="Ground" width="18" height="18" material="brown">
    <arashtad-physics type="BOX" mass="0" friction="0.8"></arashtad-physics>
</arashtad-mesh>
