What this demonstrates

A control panel that drives three independent physics interactions: spawning new bodies, applying impulses to existing ones, and resetting the scene.

Multi-Interaction

Spawn buttons add bodies anywhere on the ground with random materials. Impulse buttons grab the tower boxes by reference and push them in different directions. Control buttons clear or reset the scene.

Refs and Metadata

Pre-placed tower bodies are reached via scene.getMeshByName, then their aggregate is read from metadata.physicsAggregate. Spawned bodies are tracked in a shared array so clear and stats operations stay consistent.

Source

A control panel of native HTML buttons drives spawning, impulses, and reset operations.
