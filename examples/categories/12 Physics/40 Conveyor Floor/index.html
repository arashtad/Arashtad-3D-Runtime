Conveyor Floor

Apply continuous force to simulate a moving conveyor surface using Arashtad 3D Runtime and Havok physics.

Physics Force Kinematic
3 crates · each gets a continuous +X force of (8, 0, 0) every frame

What this demonstrates

Simulating a moving conveyor belt by applying a continuous force to bodies on the surface, since Havok's contact friction alone does not produce surface motion.

Continuous Force

Each crate has an <arashtad-action execute="..."> that registers an scene.onBeforeRenderObservable callback. Every frame the callback calls applyForce with a horizontal vector.

Friction Balance

Ground friction 0.5 is high enough that the crates stay on the surface but low enough that the continuous force actually accelerates them. End walls keep the crates from sliding off the far end.

Source

One observable per crate applies a continuous horizontal force, and static end walls bound the surface. 

<arashtad-material
    id="green"
    type="StandardMaterial"
    diffuseColor="new BABYLON.Color3(0.3, 0.5, 0.3)"
>
</arashtad-material>

<arashtad-mesh
    id="ground"
    type="Ground"
    width="24"
    height="6"
    material="brown"
>
    <arashtad-physics
        type="BOX"
        mass="0"
        friction="0.5"
    ></arashtad-physics>
</arashtad-mesh>

<arashtad-mesh
    id="crate1"
    type="Box"
    size="1"
    position="new BABYLON.Vector3(-8, 0.6, -1.5)"
    material="green"
>
    <arashtad-physics
        type="BOX"
        mass="1"
        restitution="0.1"
        friction="0.4"
    ></arashtad-physics>

    <arashtad-action
        execute="
            scene.onBeforeRenderObservable.add(() => {
                const aggregate = target.metadata?.physicsAggregate;
                if (!aggregate?.body) return;
                aggregate.body.applyForce(
                    new BABYLON.Vector3(8, 0, 0),
                    target.getAbsolutePosition()
                );
            });
        "
    >
    </arashtad-action>
</arashtad-mesh>

<!-- Repeat for crate2 (z=0), crate3 (z=1.5) -->
