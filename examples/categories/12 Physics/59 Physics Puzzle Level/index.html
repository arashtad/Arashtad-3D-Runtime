Box Puzzle

Click the green crate to push it in the direction the camera is looking. Guide it onto the gold target.

Click the green crate to push · reach the gold plate to solve

What this demonstrates

A physics-based puzzle: a clickable dynamic body, static obstacles forming a maze, a target zone, and win-condition detection driven from a per-frame check.

Click to Push

The green crate has an onPickTrigger that reads the camera's forward direction, projects it onto the ground plane, and applies an impulse in that direction. Pushing in a chosen direction is the entire input scheme.

Win Condition

Every frame the crate's horizontal distance to the gold target is measured. When it drops below a threshold, the puzzle status flips to solved. This is a per-frame check via scene.onBeforeRenderObservable.

Source

The crate pushes in the camera forward direction and the puzzle checks its proximity to the gold plate each frame. 

<arashtad-mesh
    id="crate"
    type="Box"
    size="1"
    position="new BABYLON.Vector3(-5, 0.5, 4)"
    material="green"
    onPickTrigger="
        const body = target.metadata?.physicsAggregate?.body;
        if (!body) return;

        const dir = scene.activeCamera.getForwardRay().direction.clone();
        dir.y = 0;
        dir.normalize();

        body.applyImpulse(
            dir.scale(4),
            target.getAbsolutePosition()
        );
    "
>
    <arashtad-physics
        type="BOX"
        mass="1"
        restitution="0.05"
        friction="0.5"
        linearDamping="0.6"
        angularDamping="0.6"
        startAsleep="false"
    ></arashtad-physics>
</arashtad-mesh>
