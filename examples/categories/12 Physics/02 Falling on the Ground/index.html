Falling on the Ground

Drop a dynamic box onto a static ground plane with restitution and friction using Arashtad 3D Runtime and Havok physics.

Physics Ground Basics
mass="1" · restitution="0.3" · friction="0.5" · static ground

What this demonstrates

A dynamic body colliding with a static body, and how restitution and friction shape the result.

Static Ground

A mass="0" body is static. It never falls and acts as an immovable collider for other bodies in the scene.

Restitution and Friction

restitution controls bounce, while friction controls how much the contact resists sliding. Both are per-body options.

Source

Attach a static physics body to the ground and a dynamic physics body to the box. 

<arashtad-material
    id="green"
    type="StandardMaterial"
    diffuseColor="new BABYLON.Color3(0.3, 0.5, 0.3)"
>
</arashtad-material>

<arashtad-material
    id="brown"
    type="StandardMaterial"
    diffuseColor="new BABYLON.Color3(0.7, 0.5, 0.3)"
>
</arashtad-material>

<arashtad-mesh
    id="box"
    type="Box"
    size="1"
    position="new BABYLON.Vector3(0, 5, 0)"
    material="green"
>
    <arashtad-physics
        type="BOX"
        mass="1"
        restitution="0.3"
        friction="0.5"
    ></arashtad-physics>
</arashtad-mesh>

<arashtad-mesh
    id="ground"
    type="Ground"
    width="10"
    height="10"
    material="brown"
>
    <arashtad-physics
        type="BOX"
        mass="0"
        restitution="0.2"
        friction="0.8"
    ></arashtad-physics>
</arashtad-mesh>
