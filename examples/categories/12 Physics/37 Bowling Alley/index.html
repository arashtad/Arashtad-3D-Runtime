Bowling Alley

Roll a heavy sphere into a triangle of light pins using Arashtad 3D Runtime and Havok physics.

Physics Multiple Collision
6 pins in a triangle · click the ball to roll it down the lane

What this demonstrates

A heavy sphere strikes a triangle of light cylinders, and Havok resolves the many contacts that follow.

Mass Contrast

The ball weighs mass="4" while each pin weighs mass="0.6". That ratio is what turns a small hit into a lively scatter.

Lane and Rails

The lane is a wide static ground, and two thin static rails keep the ball and pins from leaving the alley. The pins are laid out in a 1-2-3 triangle so collisions propagate through the formation.

Source

Six cylinder pins in a triangle formation, plus a heavy sphere with an onPickTrigger that rolls it down the lane. 

<arashtad-mesh
    id="lane"
    type="Ground"
    width="14"
    height="24"
    material="brown"
>
    <arashtad-physics
        type="BOX"
        mass="0"
        friction="0.7"
    ></arashtad-physics>
</arashtad-mesh>

<!-- Rails at x=±7 -->

<arashtad-mesh
    id="pin1"
    type="Cylinder"
    height="1.4"
    diameter="0.5"
    tessellation="16"
    position="new BABYLON.Vector3(0, 0.7, -8)"
    material="green"
>
    <arashtad-physics
        type="CYLINDER"
        mass="0.6"
        restitution="0.3"
        friction="0.5"
    ></arashtad-physics>
</arashtad-mesh>

<!-- pin2 (x=-1, z=-9), pin3 (x=1, z=-9) -->
<!-- pin4 (x=-2, z=-10), pin5 (x=0, z=-10), pin6 (x=2, z=-10) -->

<arashtad-mesh
    id="ball"
    type="Sphere"
    diameter="1"
    segments="24"
    position="new BABYLON.Vector3(0, 0.6, 4)"
    material="orange"
    onPickTrigger="
        const aggregate = target.metadata?.physicsAggregate;
        aggregate?.body?.setLinearVelocity(
            new BABYLON.Vector3(0, 0, -14)
        );
    "
>
    <arashtad-physics
        type="SPHERE"
        mass="4"
        restitution="0.2"
        friction="0.4"
    ></arashtad-physics>
</arashtad-mesh>
