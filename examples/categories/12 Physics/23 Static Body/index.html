Static Body

Create a static body that never moves under gravity with Arashtad 3D Runtime and Havok physics.

Physics Body Static
platform · mass="0" · static in mid-air · box · mass="1" · dynamic

What this demonstrates

A static body ignores gravity entirely and stays fixed at its starting transform, even when placed in mid-air.

Static in Mid-Air

The platform sits at y=3 with mass="0". Because a static body is not affected by gravity, it stays exactly where it was created.

Catching Dynamic Bodies

The falling box lands on the platform instead of the ground, demonstrating that a static body is a fully functional collider regardless of its position or orientation.

Source

Set mass="0" on any body to make it static. The body will not move under gravity or respond to collisions. 

<arashtad-material
    id="green"
    type="StandardMaterial"
    diffuseColor="new BABYLON.Color3(0.3, 0.5, 0.3)"
>
</arashtad-material>

<arashtad-material
    id="blue"
    type="StandardMaterial"
    diffuseColor="new BABYLON.Color3(0.3, 0.5, 0.9)"
>
</arashtad-material>

<arashtad-material
    id="brown"
    type="StandardMaterial"
    diffuseColor="new BABYLON.Color3(0.7, 0.5, 0.3)"
>
</arashtad-material>

<arashtad-mesh
    id="ground"
    type="Ground"
    width="14"
    height="14"
    material="brown"
>
    <arashtad-physics
        type="BOX"
        mass="0"
        friction="0.8"
    ></arashtad-physics>
</arashtad-mesh>

<arashtad-mesh
    id="platform"
    type="Box"
    width="4"
    height="0.4"
    depth="2"
    position="new BABYLON.Vector3(0, 3, 0)"
    material="blue"
>
    <arashtad-physics
        type="BOX"
        mass="0"
        friction="0.6"
    ></arashtad-physics>
</arashtad-mesh>

<arashtad-mesh
    id="box"
    type="Box"
    size="1"
    position="new BABYLON.Vector3(0, 8, 0)"
    material="green"
>
    <arashtad-physics
        type="BOX"
        mass="1"
        restitution="0.3"
        friction="0.5"
    ></arashtad-physics>
</arashtad-mesh>
