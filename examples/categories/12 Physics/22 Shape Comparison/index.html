Shape Comparison

Compare BOX, SPHERE, CAPSULE, and CYLINDER physics shapes side by side with Arashtad 3D Runtime and Havok physics.

Physics Shape Comparison
BOX · SPHERE · CAPSULE · CYLINDER · same mass, restitution, friction

What this demonstrates

Four shapes with identical physics settings, dropped side by side, so their differences are visible at a glance.

Same Settings, Different Shapes

Every body uses mass="1", restitution="0.3", and friction="0.5". Because the mesh factory and the physics type both change between bodies, any behavioral difference on landing comes purely from the shape itself.

Shape-Specific Behavior

The sphere rolls, the cylinder may tip or roll, the capsule settles upright thanks to its rounded ends, and the box lands on a flat face. The differences emerge naturally from the collider geometry.

Source

Match each mesh factory with its analytic Havok shape and keep the physical parameters identical between bodies.
