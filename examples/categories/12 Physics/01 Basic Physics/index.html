151 · PHYSICS

Basic Fall

Drop a single dynamic box and watch it respond to gravity with Arashtad 3D Runtime and Havok physics.

Physics Basics Havok

What this demonstrates

The simplest possible physics setup: enable Havok, attach a physics body to a mesh, and let gravity do the rest.

Havok Physics

The presence of the <arashtad-physics> element lazily initializes Havok physics on the scene using standard gravity.

Dynamic Body

A positive mass value makes the box a dynamic body, so it falls under gravity and responds to collisions.

Source

Attach a physics body to any mesh with the <arashtad-physics> element. 

<arashtad-material
    id="green"
    type="StandardMaterial"
    diffuseColor="new BABYLON.Color3(0.3, 0.5, 0.3)"
>
</arashtad-material>

<arashtad-mesh
    id="box"
    type="Box"
    size="1"
    position="new BABYLON.Vector3(0, 5, 0)"
    material="green"
>
    <arashtad-physics
        type="BOX"
        mass="1"
        restitution="0.3"
        friction="0.5"
    ></arashtad-physics>
</arashtad-mesh>
