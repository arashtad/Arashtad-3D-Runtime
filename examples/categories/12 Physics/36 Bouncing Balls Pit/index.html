Bouncing Balls Pit

Drop many spheres of different restitution into a bowl with Arashtad 3D Runtime and Havok physics.

Physics Multiple Restitution
5 balls · restitution 0.3 → 0.95 · dropped into a bowl of static walls

What this demonstrates

Restitution directly controls bounce height. Dropping several balls into a shared bowl makes the difference immediate.

Restitution Range

The five balls use restitution values of 0.3, 0.5, 0.6, 0.85, and 0.95. The first barely bounces, while the last keeps rebounding for many seconds.

Shared Bowl

Four thin static walls surround the ground plane, forming a bowl. Balls collide with the walls and with each other, producing a lively bouncing scene without anything escaping.

Source

Static walls form the bowl, and each ball carries its own restitution value. 

<arashtad-material
    id="brown"
    type="StandardMaterial"
    diffuseColor="new BABYLON.Color3(0.7, 0.5, 0.3)"
>
</arashtad-material>

<arashtad-mesh
    id="bowlbottom"
    type="Ground"
    width="8"
    height="8"
    material="brown"
>
    <arashtad-physics
        type="BOX"
        mass="0"
        restitution="0.6"
        friction="0.4"
    ></arashtad-physics>
</arashtad-mesh>

<arashtad-mesh
    id="wallleft"
    type="Box"
    width="0.4"
    height="3"
    depth="8"
    position="new BABYLON.Vector3(-4, 1.5, 0)"
    material="brown"
>
    <arashtad-physics
        type="BOX"
        mass="0"
        restitution="0.4"
        friction="0.6"
    ></arashtad-physics>
</arashtad-mesh>

<!-- Repeat for wallright, wallback, wallfront -->

<arashtad-mesh
    id="ball1"
    type="Sphere"
    diameter="0.8"
    segments="24"
    position="new BABYLON.Vector3(-1.5, 8, -1)"
    material="green"
>
    <arashtad-physics
        type="SPHERE"
        mass="1"
        restitution="0.3"
        friction="0.4"
    ></arashtad-physics>
</arashtad-mesh>
