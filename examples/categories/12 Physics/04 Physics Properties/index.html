Physics Properties

Configure restitution, friction, linear damping, and angular damping on a physics body with Arashtad 3D Runtime and Havok physics.

Physics Properties Havok
restitution="0.3" · friction="0.5" · linearDamping="0.5" · angularDamping="0.5"

What this demonstrates

The four per-body properties that shape how a dynamic body behaves on contact and over time.

Restitution and Friction

restitution sets how bouncy the body is on contact, and friction sets how much it resists sliding along surfaces it touches.

Linear and Angular Damping

linearDamping slows translational motion over time, and angularDamping slows rotation. Higher values make the body settle faster.

Source

All four properties are configured as attributes on <arashtad-physics>. 

<arashtad-material
    id="green"
    type="StandardMaterial"
    diffuseColor="new BABYLON.Color3(0.3, 0.5, 0.3)"
>
</arashtad-material>

<arashtad-material
    id="brown"
    type="StandardMaterial"
    diffuseColor="new BABYLON.Color3(0.7, 0.5, 0.3)"
>
</arashtad-material>

<arashtad-mesh
    id="box"
    type="Box"
    size="1"
    position="new BABYLON.Vector3(0, 5, 0)"
    material="green"
>
    <arashtad-physics
        type="BOX"
        mass="1"
        restitution="0.3"
        friction="0.5"
        linearDamping="0.5"
        angularDamping="0.5"
    ></arashtad-physics>
</arashtad-mesh>

<arashtad-mesh
    id="ground"
    type="Ground"
    width="10"
    height="10"
    material="brown"
>
    <arashtad-physics
        type="BOX"
        mass="0"
        friction="0.8"
    ></arashtad-physics>
</arashtad-mesh>
