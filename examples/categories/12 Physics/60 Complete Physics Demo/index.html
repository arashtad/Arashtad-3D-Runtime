What this demonstrates

Every major physics capability the runtime supports, in a single interactive scene.

What's Included

Falling (spawned bodies drop from above), stacking (a pyramid and a tower stand under gravity), shooting (a heavy gold ball fires at the pyramid), collision UI (live stats update on impact), and pause/reset controls.

State Management

Static bodies (walls, platform, tower base) are placed in markup. Dynamic bodies (pyramid, tower, cannonball) are also in markup. Spawned bodies are tracked in JS for cleanup and stats.

Source

Arena, walls, platform, pyramid, tower, and cannonball are declared in markup. Spawning, shooting, pause, and reset are driven from a host-page control panel. 

<arashtad-mesh id="ground" type="Ground" width="30" height="24" material="brown">
    <arashtad-physics type="BOX" mass="0" friction="0.8"></arashtad-physics>
</arashtad-mesh>
