Impulse at Contact Point

Compare an off-center impulse against a centered one with Arashtad 3D Runtime and Havok physics.

Physics Impulse Contact
value="(6, 0, 0)" · contact at center · contact above center

What this demonstrates

The same impulse value applied at two different world-space contact points produces very different rotation responses.

Centered Contact

When contact matches the body's center of mass, the impulse produces pure translation with no rotation. The green box slides away without spinning.

Off-Center Contact

When contact is offset from the center, part of the impulse becomes torque. The orange box slides and tumbles because the contact sits above the center line.

Source

Keep the impulse value identical and only move the contact point to isolate the effect of the offset. 

<arashtad-material
    id="green"
    type="StandardMaterial"
    diffuseColor="new BABYLON.Color3(0.3, 0.5, 0.3)"
>
</arashtad-material>

<arashtad-material
    id="orange"
    type="StandardMaterial"
    diffuseColor="new BABYLON.Color3(1.0, 0.4, 0.3)"
>
</arashtad-material>

<arashtad-mesh
    id="centered"
    type="Box"
    size="1"
    position="new BABYLON.Vector3(-2.5, 1, 0)"
    material="green"
>
    <arashtad-physics
        type="BOX"
        mass="1"
        restitution="0.1"
        friction="0.4"
    >
        <arashtad-impulse
            value="new BABYLON.Vector3(6, 0, 0)"
            contact="new BABYLON.Vector3(-2.5, 1, 0)"
        ></arashtad-impulse>
    </arashtad-physics>
</arashtad-mesh>

    </arashtad-physics>
