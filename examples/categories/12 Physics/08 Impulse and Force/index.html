Impulse and Force

Combine an impulse with a continuous force on the same body using Arashtad 3D Runtime and Havok physics.

Physics Impulse Force
impulse (0, 8, 0) · force (20, 0, 0) · contact (0, 5, 0)

What this demonstrates

Stack multiple force-like elements inside one <arashtad-physics> block to combine an instantaneous kick with a continuous push.

Impulse vs Force

An <arashtad-impulse> applies a one-shot change of momentum. A <arashtad-force> applies a continuous force that acts across simulation frames.

Contact Point

Both elements accept a contact attribute that determines where in world space the effect is applied, so an off-center contact can introduce rotation.

Source

Combine <arashtad-impulse> with <arashtad-force> inside the same <arashtad-physics>. 

<arashtad-material
    id="green"
    type="StandardMaterial"
    diffuseColor="new BABYLON.Color3(0.3, 0.5, 0.3)"
>
</arashtad-material>

<arashtad-mesh
    id="box"
    type="Box"
    size="1"
    position="new BABYLON.Vector3(0, 2, 0)"
    material="green"
>
    <arashtad-physics
        type="BOX"
        mass="1"
    >
        <arashtad-impulse
            value="new BABYLON.Vector3(0, 8, 0)"
            contact="new BABYLON.Vector3(0, 2, 0)"
        ></arashtad-impulse>

        <arashtad-force
            value="new BABYLON.Vector3(20, 0, 0)"
            contact="new BABYLON.Vector3(0, 5, 0)"
        ></arashtad-force>
    </arashtad-physics>
</arashtad-mesh>
