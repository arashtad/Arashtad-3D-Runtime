Collision Impulse Meter

Visualize the last collision impulse magnitude with a meter using Arashtad 3D Runtime and Havok physics.

Two boxes with different mass · impulse magnitude compared per collision

What this demonstrates

Read the impulse magnitude Havok reports on each collision and use it to drive a visual meter. Heavy vs light bodies produce visibly different impact readings.

Reading the Impulse

The collision event exposes event.impulse. Its exact shape varies, so the meter helper inspects x, y, z fields when present, or falls back to length() if the value happens to be a Vector3.

Mass Contrast

The heavy box (mass="3") produces a much larger impulse on impact than the light box (mass="0.4"). Watching the meter between the two shows that mass directly scales impact magnitude.

Source

Both boxes funnel their collision events into a shared helper that computes magnitude and drives the meter. 

<arashtad-mesh
    id="heavybox"
    type="Box"
    size="1.4"
    position="new BABYLON.Vector3(-2, 8, 0)"
    material="green"
>
    <arashtad-physics
        type="BOX"
        mass="3"
        restitution="0.3"
        friction="0.6"
        startAsleep="false"
    >
        <arashtad-collision
            type="started"
            on-collide="
                window.__updateImpulseMeter('heavy', event);
            "
        ></arashtad-collision>
    </arashtad-physics>
</arashtad-mesh>

<arashtad-mesh
    id="lightbox"
    type="Box"
    size="0.8"
    position="new BABYLON.Vector3(2, 5, 0)"
    material="orange"
>
    <arashtad-physics
        type="BOX"
        mass="0.4"
        restitution="0.6"
        friction="0.4"
        startAsleep="false"
    >
        <arashtad-collision
            type="started"
            on-collide="
                window.__updateImpulseMeter('light', event);
            "
        ></arashtad-collision>
    </arashtad-physics>
</arashtad-mesh>
