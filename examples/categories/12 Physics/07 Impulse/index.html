Impulse

Kick a resting box upward with an impulse at a contact point using Arashtad 3D Runtime and Havok physics.

Physics Impulse Havok
value="(0, 8, 0)" · contact="(0, 2, 0)"

What this demonstrates

Apply a one-shot impulse to a physics body through a nested <arashtad-impulse> element.

Impulse Value

value is the impulse vector. An upward (0, 8, 0) kick launches the box against gravity in a single frame.

Contact Point

contact is the world-space point where the impulse is applied. When it is offset from the body's center, the impulse also produces rotation.

Source

Nest <arashtad-impulse> inside <arashtad-physics> to apply a one-shot impulse to the body. 

<arashtad-material
    id="green"
    type="StandardMaterial"
    diffuseColor="new BABYLON.Color3(0.3, 0.5, 0.3)"
>
</arashtad-material>

<arashtad-mesh
    id="box"
    type="Box"
    size="1"
    position="new BABYLON.Vector3(0, 2, 0)"
    material="green"
>
    <arashtad-physics
        type="BOX"
        mass="1"
    >
        <arashtad-impulse
            value="new BABYLON.Vector3(0, 8, 0)"
            contact="new BABYLON.Vector3(0, 2, 0)"
        ></arashtad-impulse>
    </arashtad-physics>
</arashtad-mesh>
