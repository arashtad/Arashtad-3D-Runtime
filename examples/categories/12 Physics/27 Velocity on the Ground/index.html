Velocity on the Ground

Give a resting box horizontal velocity and watch friction slow it down using Arashtad 3D Runtime and Havok physics.

Physics Velocity Friction
same launch velocity · green friction="0.1" · orange friction="0.9"

What this demonstrates

Two bodies launched with the same horizontal velocity, but different friction values, so the ground slows them at different rates.

Initial Velocity

A nested <arashtad-velocity> gives each box a horizontal velocity of (6, 0, 0) at spawn. That is the entire push the body receives.

Friction Contrast

Friction converts horizontal motion into heat once the body rests on the ground. The green box glides for a long distance, while the orange box stops almost immediately.

Source

Two boxes with identical initial velocity and different friction values on their physics bodies. 

<arashtad-material
    id="green"
    type="StandardMaterial"
    diffuseColor="new BABYLON.Color3(0.3, 0.5, 0.3)"
>
</arashtad-material>

<arashtad-material
    id="orange"
    type="StandardMaterial"
    diffuseColor="new BABYLON.Color3(1.0, 0.4, 0.3)"
>
</arashtad-material>

<arashtad-mesh
    id="lowfriction"
    type="Box"
    size="1"
    position="new BABYLON.Vector3(-6, 0.5, -1.5)"
    material="green"
>
    <arashtad-physics
        type="BOX"
        mass="1"
        restitution="0.1"
        friction="0.1"
    >
        <arashtad-velocity
            linear="new BABYLON.Vector3(6, 0, 0)"
        ></arashtad-velocity>
    </arashtad-physics>
</arashtad-mesh>

<arashtad-mesh
    id="highfriction"
    type="Box"
    size="1"
    position="new BABYLON.Vector3(-6, 0.5, 1.5)"
    material="orange"
>
    <arashtad-physics
        type="BOX"
        mass="1"
        restitution="0.1"
        friction="0.9"
    >
        <arashtad-velocity
            linear="new BABYLON.Vector3(6, 0, 0)"
        ></arashtad-velocity>
    </arashtad-physics>
</arashtad-mesh>
