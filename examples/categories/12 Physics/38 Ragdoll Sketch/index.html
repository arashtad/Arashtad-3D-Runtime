Ragdoll Sketch

Chain multiple bodies with compounds to approximate a ragdoll using Arashtad 3D Runtime and Havok physics.

Physics Multiple Compound
6 chained bodies · click the sphere to knock the ragdoll over

What this demonstrates

Several independent bodies stacked in roughly humanoid proportions, each with its own mass and physics shape, so they topple and tumble as a loose group.

Compound Approximation

A true compound body would fuse these shapes into one rigid mesh. Here, each part is its own body with matched damping, so they move together in free flight and collapse loosely once they hit the ground.

Matched Parameters

Every part uses the same friction, restitution, and damping so the group's overall motion feels like one object. Masses vary by role — the torso is heaviest and the arms are lightest.

Source

Six independent bodies with matched physics parameters, positioned in roughly humanoid proportions. 

<arashtad-mesh
    id="torso"
    type="Capsule"
    height="1.4"
    radius="0.4"
    position="new BABYLON.Vector3(0, 4, 0)"
    material="blue"
>
    <arashtad-physics
        type="CAPSULE"
        mass="2"
        restitution="0.1"
        friction="0.6"
        linearDamping="0.3"
        angularDamping="0.3"
    ></arashtad-physics>
</arashtad-mesh>

<!-- Legs (Capsule, mass 0.7 each) -->

<arashtad-mesh
    id="pusher"
    type="Sphere"
    diameter="0.9"
    position="new BABYLON.Vector3(-6, 4, 0)"
    material="green"
    onPickTrigger="
        const aggregate = target.metadata?.physicsAggregate;
        aggregate?.body?.setLinearVelocity(
            new BABYLON.Vector3(12, 0, 0)
        );
    "
>
    <arashtad-physics
        type="SPHERE"
        mass="3"
        restitution="0.2"
        friction="0.4"
    ></arashtad-physics>
</arashtad-mesh>
