Physics Shape Cylinder

Use a CYLINDER physics shape for barrels and pillars with Arashtad 3D Runtime and Havok physics.

Physics Shape Havok
type="CYLINDER" · three barrels with staggered heights

What this demonstrates

Give a cylinder-shaped mesh a matching CYLINDER physics shape, the collider of choice for barrels, pillars, and pipes.

CYLINDER Shape

A cylinder collider is a right circular cylinder with flat end caps. It fits any mesh whose cross section is round, such as a barrel or a pillar.

Staggered Heights

Three barrels start at different heights so they land at different times and don't overlap. This keeps the collision resolution clean and the demo easy to read.

Source

Match the mesh type with the physics shape type. Cylinder meshes pair with type="CYLINDER" on the nested <arashtad-physics>. 

<arashtad-material
    id="green"
    type="StandardMaterial"

<arashtad-material

<arashtad-mesh
    id="ground"
    type="Ground"
    width="14"

    material="brown"
>
    <arashtad-physics
