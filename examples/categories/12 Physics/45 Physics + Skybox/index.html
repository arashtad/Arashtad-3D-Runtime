Physics + Skybox

Fall a box inside an environment skybox with HDR reflections using Arashtad 3D Runtime and Havok physics.

Physics Environment HDR
HDR environment skybox · two PBR boxes falling under gravity

What this demonstrates

Physics and environment lighting compose freely. A metallic PBR body reflects the HDR skybox while falling under Havok gravity.

HDR Environment

The <arashtad-runtime environment="room.hdr"> loads an HDR cube texture, sets it as the scene environment, and creates a skybox. Metallic PBR surfaces automatically sample it for reflections.

PBR + Physics

Two boxes with different metallic finishes fall and bounce on the ground. The gold box has a warm albedo and mid roughness, while the chrome box is nearly a mirror. Both reflect the room environment as they tumble.

Source

Combine an HDR environment on the runtime element with PBR metallic materials on physics bodies. 

<arashtad-runtime
    lazy
    resize="engine.resize()"
    environment="../../../assets/environments/room.hdr"
    environmentIntensity="1"
    environmentBackground="true"
    environmentBackgroundBlur="0.1"
    environmentRotationY="1"
>
</arashtad-runtime>

<arashtad-material
    id="gold"
    type="PBRMaterial"
    metallic="1"
    roughness="0.15"
    albedoColor="new BABYLON.Color3(1.0, 0.78, 0.34)"
>
</arashtad-material>

<arashtad-material
    id="chrome"
    type="PBRMaterial"
    metallic="1"
    roughness="0.05"
    albedoColor="new BABYLON.Color3(0.85, 0.85, 0.9)"
>
</arashtad-material>

<arashtad-mesh
    id="goldbox"
    type="Box"
    size="1.2"
    position="new BABYLON.Vector3(-1.5, 6, 0)"
    material="gold"
>
    <arashtad-physics
        type="BOX"
        mass="1"
        restitution="0.4"
        friction="0.5"
        startAsleep="false"
    ></arashtad-physics>
</arashtad-mesh>

<arashtad-mesh
    id="chromebox"
    type="Box"
    size="1.2"
    position="new BABYLON.Vector3(1.5, 8, 0)"
    material="chrome"
>
    <arashtad-physics
        type="BOX"
        mass="1"
        restitution="0.6"
        friction="0.5"
        startAsleep="false"
    ></arashtad-physics>
</arashtad-mesh>
