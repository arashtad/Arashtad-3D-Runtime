Basic Material Properties

A StandardMaterial can be created independently and assigned to one or more runtime meshes.

Material

Type - StandardMaterial

Diffuse Color - Teal

Specular Color - Light gray

Assigned To - Box mesh

Source

The material is declared independently and referenced by the mesh through its material attribute. 

<arashtad-material
    type="StandardMaterial"
    id="box-material"
    diffuseColor="new BABYLON.Color3(0.15, 0.64, 0.55)"
    specularColor="new BABYLON.Color3(0.8, 0.8, 0.8)">
</arashtad-material>

<arashtad-mesh
    type="Box"
    id="material-box"
    size="2.5"
    position="new BABYLON.Vector3(0, 1.25, 0)"
    material="box-material">
</arashtad-mesh>
